A raw spreadsheet can be useful, but it quickly becomes hard to scan. Joya Grid turns the Joyagoo spreadsheet idea into a cleaner, crawlable guide with category routes, product cards, update notes, and practical buying checks.

What the Joyagoo Spreadsheet is for

The Joyagoo Spreadsheet is best used as a discovery layer. It helps you compare product types, learn the right search terms, and decide which categories are worth opening before you spend time reviewing individual listings.

How to browse it

Start with a broad category such as sneakers, hoodies, outerwear, pants, bags, accessories, or tees. After that, narrow by silhouette, color, material, season, and fit. This keeps the first pass visual and prevents the common mistake of copying weak product titles into search.

What to check before saving a find

Do not treat a spreadsheet row as a buying decision. Check measurements, QC photos, product details, seller notes, estimated item weight, and likely shipping cost. A find is useful only when it survives those checks.
