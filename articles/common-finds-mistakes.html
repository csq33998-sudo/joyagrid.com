Mistakes

Common mistakes when browsing finds and sheets

Finds sites and spreadsheets are useful, but they can also encourage fast, unfocused decisions. Avoid these common Joyagoo spreadsheet mistakes before building a cart, trusting a Reddit mention, or saving a product only because it looks cheap.

Trusting old links without checking

Links can go stale. Open the destination, confirm the page loads, and compare similar results before assuming a find is still active.

Searching with one vague word

Words like shoes, hoodie, or bag are starting points, not final searches. Add silhouette, color, material, or use case for better results.

Ignoring measurements

Streetwear fit depends on shape. If measurements are available, compare them with clothing you already own.

Skipping QC review

QC photos are where many issues become visible. Check shape, color, stitching, material, and packaging before final shipment.

If a spreadsheet row claims strong QC but the photos do not show the important details, keep researching. The QC photo examples on the main hub show what to check by category.

Adding too many filler items

Small accessories and cheap tees can make a haul feel bigger, but they also add weight and clutter. Save items that have a clear outfit role.

Ignoring landed cost

A cheap find can become expensive after service fees, weight, international shipping, and exchange buffer. Run heavier items through the agent shopping cost calculator before calling them a best item.

Trusting community hype without verification

Reddit-style comments, haul posts, and Discord discussions can reveal useful concerns, but they are not a substitute for checking the current product page, QC photos, sizing, and cost. Use community sources as prompts for what to verify, not as proof that a find is safe.

Browse with a plan

Start with one category and narrow from there.
