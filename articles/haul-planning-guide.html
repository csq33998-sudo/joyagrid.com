Haul planning

Joyagoo haul planning guide for spreadsheet finds

A good haul starts smaller than most beginners expect. Use the Joyagoo spreadsheet 2026 hub to discover options, then reduce the list by fit confidence, QC-photo quality, landed cost, weight, and daily outfit use.

Start with one outfit direction

Choose one practical direction before opening dozens of links: daily sneakers and pants, a hoodie-focused casual outfit, a jacket layer for colder weather, or a small accessories refresh. A clear direction stops the haul from becoming a random collection of cheap finds.

Separate must-have items from filler

Put every spreadsheet find into one of three groups: core outfit item, useful support item, or filler. Core items deserve the most QC review. Support items should solve a real styling need. Filler items should usually be removed unless the cost and weight are minimal.

Estimate landed cost before the cart grows

Item price is only the first number. Use the agent shopping cost calculator to estimate domestic shipping, service fee, international shipping, coupons, and exchange buffer. This is especially important for sneakers, denim, jackets, and heavy hoodies.

Use QC photos as the final filter

Before a find stays in the haul, check shape, measurements, fabric, stitching, color, print placement, tags, hardware, sole shape, and packaging. If a detail matters to fit or daily use and the QC photos do not show it, keep the item in research mode.
