Joyagoo spreadsheet FAQ

Common questions

What is the Joyagoo Spreadsheet?

It is an independent research guide with a small set of dated MaisonLooks source records, direct product links, and explicit QC status. It does not perform first-hand product tests.

Is Joya Grid official?

No. Joya Grid is an independent editorial site. It does not sell items, process orders, or represent an official Joyagoo service.

What should I check before buying?

Compare measurements, QC photos, seller details, product images, estimated weight, shipping fees, and whether the item matches your intended fit.

Before you buy

Use the source page for the final check

JoyaGrid helps you compare product finds and QC photos. Prices, availability, options, and external policies can change, so confirm the latest details on the linked source page before ordering.
