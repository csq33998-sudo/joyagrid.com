Source policy

How community sources are used

Community posts, Reddit-style discussions, haul notes, and QC conversations can raise questions worth investigating. Joya Grid does not copy user comments as original content; it turns recurring concerns into practical verification checklists.

Review method

What an editorial check includes

Editors compare the board entry with the current destination, then look for useful product context: recognizable category, readable photos, price, available options, measurements, and QC questions a buyer should ask. A listing can still change after that review, so every page encourages visitors to confirm the live destination before ordering. Inclusion means the find may be worth researching; it is not a guarantee of quality, authenticity, availability, or delivery.

Corrections

How changes and mistakes are handled

Dated notes describe completed editorial work, not automatic proof that every external listing is current. When a material error or changed destination is identified, the relevant description, warning, or route should be corrected rather than hidden behind a new date. Readers can send a concise correction with the page URL, disputed text, and supporting evidence to editor@joyagrid.com.

Limits

What Joya Grid cannot verify

Product cards and photos cannot establish comfort, long-term durability, material composition, seller conduct, agent policy, or final landed cost. External services control their own listings, payments, storage, shipping, returns, and support. Joya Grid therefore focuses on organizing evidence and explaining checks while leaving the purchase decision and risk assessment with the visitor.
