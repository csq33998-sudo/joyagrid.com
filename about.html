About Joya Grid

Independent editorial

Built as a research layer, not an official store

Joya Grid is an independent editorial discovery site. It uses a JoyaGoo spreadsheet style format to organize curated categories, searchable outfit cards, QC-photo prompts, sizing notes, cost planning, and outbound research paths. We do not sell products, process payments, or represent Joyagoo, Pandabuy, sellers, or agents.

How community sources are used
