Calculator FAQ

Use it before you commit

Why include weight?

International shipping often changes the real price more than the item price. Estimate weight early so you can compare finds fairly.

Why add a buffer?

Payment fees, currency conversion, and small service charges can move the final total. A buffer keeps your estimate realistic.

What should I check next?

Review QC photos, measurements, seller notes, return terms, and whether the final landed cost still makes sense.
